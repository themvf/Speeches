# Voice quality and collection, v1

Voice roles use saved evidence and reproducible text heuristics. They do not certify influence, human authorship, independent audiences, profitable calls, or factual accuracy.

- Meme-token early ranking requires an exact-contract anchor; older name-only records remain stored but do not qualify. After-anchor name matches remain provisional. Native Zcash accepts bare ZEC and Zcash and has no invented contract/launch anchor.
- Roles distinguish early discoverers (first seven days after anchor), original-analysis candidates, amplifiers, configured project/platform sources, and probable reporting feeds. Repeated formats do not increase analysis credit. Quote/reply account counts are distinct within an author, not independent or necessarily supportive people.
- Existing watcher ranking timestamps are normalized before grouping and sorting.

## Collection

Existing 150,000-credit rolling campaign (30,000 per coin), 150,000-credit watcher campaign, expiry dates, daily cap and global uncertain-request lock remain in force. This change never resets those ledgers.

Four rolling search pages per coin/run: first prioritizes a fresh window, next two prefer the oldest partial window, fourth prefers focused identity searches. Unused lanes fall back to available work. Focus searches cover 30 one-hour windows beginning six hours before the earliest saved contract post. They are not a promise of complete historical coverage. Old broad unfinished PONS/STANDARD rolling queries are retired with their evidence retained. New queries omit ambiguous standalone $PONS/$STANDARD search terms.

Two watcher pages/account/run: first prioritizes fresh, second prefers oldest partial. No automatic expansion of the fixed watcher list.

A daily engagement refresh selects up to ten saved original posts currently 24–30 hours old. A conservative 300-credit reservation is reconciled to the validated response. Daily and campaign ceilings may prevent work. These snapshots provide comparable-age evidence for the existing engagement metrics.

## Weekly cohorts and prospective evaluation

`crypto_voice_snapshots` freezes a cohort on first execution that week. Selection draws up to five each from large audiences, early discoverers, original analysis and amplifiers, avoiding duplicates and filling shortages from amplifiers. Automated/project source candidates are excluded. The resulting maximum 20 accounts are refreshed daily within the existing allowance. Follower snapshots remain in existing profile history.

Follower-count and posting-volume baseline groups are frozen at the same time. After seven days, evaluation records future collected posts and incoming accounts for all members, including inactive ones. Only posts captured before the evaluation horizon can contribute. Evaluation is immutable. It measures sampled subsequent attention, not returns or causal influence, and unequal collection/identity precision can bias comparisons. Comparisons with fewer than 20 candidates must account for group size and overlap.

No synthetic growth or prospective results are shown before observations exist. The first cohort starts when this release runs, not retroactively at Monday midnight. Future snapshots retain the actual creation time and model version.
